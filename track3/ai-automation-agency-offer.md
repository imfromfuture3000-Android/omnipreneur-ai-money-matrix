# Track 3: AI Automation Agency Lite – Offer & Delivery System

## Positioning
“We install done-for-you AI content systems and 24/7 lead responders for creators and local service businesses so you stop losing time and money on manual work.”

## Core Offers

### Offer A: Done-for-You AI Content System
- Full Content Matrix setup (Notion + prompts + Make.com automation)
- 30-day content calendar
- First 10 scripts + 20 Shorts generated
- Training video + SOP
- Pricing: $2,500 setup + $500/mo optimization & new assets

### Offer B: 24/7 AI Lead Responder + Booking Agent
- Custom GPT / Claude agent or Make.com + GHL / Calendly integration
- SMS + Email follow-up sequences
- Missed-call text-back
- Review request automation
- Pricing: $1,800–$3,500 setup + $400–$800/mo

### Offer C: Full Omnipreneur Stack (Premium)
Both A + B + monthly strategy call
$5,000 setup + $1,000/mo

## Ideal Clients
- YouTube / TikTok creators doing 5k–100k views/month who want scale without burnout
- Local service businesses (HVAC, dental, roofing, clinics, agencies) with website + Google Business Profile
- Solopreneurs already making $5k+/mo who are time-constrained

## Sales Process
1. Free 20-min “AI Opportunity Audit” (Zoom)
2. Show 2–3 specific automations they are missing
3. Present package with ROI calculation (hours saved × hourly rate)
4. Close with 50% deposit, rest on delivery
5. Onboard → deliver in 7–14 days → hand off with SOPs
